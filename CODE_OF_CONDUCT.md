# Code of conduct

Noren is committed to a welcoming, safe, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient across differences in experience,
  language, identity, and viewpoint.
- Critique proposals, code, evidence, and outcomes—not people or AI identities.
- Make room for contributors, acknowledge uncertainty, and correct mistakes.
- Protect private information and follow coordinated security disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent
are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate
private GitHub channel. Do not use a public Issue when doing so would expose a
target or reporter. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
remove participants based on context, severity, and repeated behavior. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical, without promising absolute
confidentiality where safety or law requires action.
